Digital Sales & Marketing Executive Intern

Help us put SentinelFlux and its products in front of the right teams. You will run outbound and inbound campaigns, qualify leads and support the founder in turning trials into customers.

What you'll do
  • Research and build prospect lists across SaaS, IT services and product companies

Who you are
  • MBA or BBA (pursuing or completed)

  • Comfortable with cold outreach, follow-ups and handling objections
  • Curiosity about SaaS and AI products; basic familiarity with LinkedIn, Google Workspace and a CRM or spreadsheets
Stipend after month 3: ₹1,000 / month + conversion incentives

AI QA Intern

Work on the quality of our AI products. You will design test scenarios, run them, and help evaluate how AI agents behave — alongside the team building VigilQA.

What you'll do
  • Write and execute functional, regression and exploratory tests on our web products
  • Create test cases and automate them with tools such as Playwright
  • Evaluate AI outputs for accuracy, consistency and edge-case failures
  • Log clear, reproducible bugs and verify fixes
  • Use AI tools to speed up test design and triage
Who you are
  • BE / B.Tech in CSE, AI, or Data Science (pursuing or completed)
  • Good grasp of testing fundamentals and SDLC
  • Basic programming in Python or JavaScript
  • Attention to detail and clear written communication
Stipend after month 3: ₹2,000 / month
